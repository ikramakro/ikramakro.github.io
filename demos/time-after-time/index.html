Seoul-influenced viennoiserie, on a Melbourne laneway.

Time After Time is at 7 Howey Place, Melbourne. Broadsheet describes charcoal croissants inspired by Seoul bakeries, from chef and owner Mino Han. The inky pastry is mostly for aesthetics; the runaway hit is a Melbourne-brunch version topped with ricotta, berry coulis, fresh figs, and honey, alongside matcha and black-cocoa versions. Press and directories still point to Instagram, not an official website.
