Ikigai Coffee

A calm coffee shop run by a Hong Kong couple, a short walk from the station: unsweetened Japanese matcha, espresso, and cakes baked on the premises.

Near the station

Easy to miss on a Tuesday. Easy to love the rest of the week.

Ikigai sits at 37 Queens Road, close to Brighton station and North Laine. Public listings show a Google rating around 4.9 from 367–483 reviews, and a Food Standards Agency hygiene rating of 5 (inspection 19 October 2023). Reviewers often mention matcha, cakes made on site, and friendly service in a clean, cozy room.
