Artisan bakery-cafe

A small shop that’s easier to find when the hours are clear.

Kuna is a compact bakery-cafe on Elm Row, loved for pastries made from scratch and a friendly counter. There is no booking — just walk in. Indoor and outdoor seats are available, and dogs are welcome. Public listings show a 4.8 rating from about 367 Google reviews, including reviews from 2026.
