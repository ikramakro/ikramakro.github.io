Chinatown

Already in the press. Still without a homepage.

Loreto Brunch and Dinner opened at 219 Union Street, in a space that previously held The Union and La Cerveceria Astilleros. Daily Hive (24 June 2026) described a Mexican-inspired brunch and dinner menu, and said the restaurant is open daily from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Search still finds Instagram, press, and directories — not an official website.
