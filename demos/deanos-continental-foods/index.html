Stratford station exit

Hard to find online. Hard to miss once you step outside.

Deano’s Continental Foods sits at 62b Montfichet Road, opposite the Stratford Tube and train exit. My London (27 July 2025) described a bright green vintage-signed stall with friendly, fast service and no website or social media. A food-hygiene listing for “Deano” in Stratford also exists on the Food Standards Agency site. This page is only a demo concept, so people can look up the stall before they travel.

From a July 2025 review

Classic, cheap, and cooked in front of you.

The snapshot below comes from My London’s visit on 27 July 2025. Prices may have changed. One later public review also mentioned that this seller has been cash only — please be prepared, and confirm on the day.
