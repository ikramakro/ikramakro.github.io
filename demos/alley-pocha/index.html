Already in the press — still without a menu page.

Alley Pocha (골목포차) opened on 1 June 2026 at 690 Euclid Avenue, in the former Thay Bar space, a side street off Bloor rather than a Bloor storefront. BlogTO covered the opening. Very Toronto (re-checked 10 September 2026) still lists Instagram as the official channel and says there is no marketing website. The room is walk-in only.
