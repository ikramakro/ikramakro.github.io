Hong Kong comfort, Manchester setting

Cha chaan teng energy on Great Ducie Street.

Happy Valley Cafe serves Hong Kong café classics with a few fusion plates, in a colourful room that’s been written up for its cartoon murals and pavement tables. It sits near Strangeways, a short walk from Victoria. The Food Standards Agency awarded a hygiene rating of 5 (inspection 20 September 2023).
