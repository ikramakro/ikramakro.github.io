Downtown Vancouver

A short-term cafe with a long line of matcha fans.

Unscripted Coffee is a new cafe from sisters Angel and Sara Dee, steps from Waterfront Station. Daily Hive described a scrappy, warehouse-inspired room with milk-crate tables and a sticky-note menu. Noms later reported the cafe would stay open through the end of 2026 — please confirm on Instagram, because the run has been discussed as a short lease. Search still finds Instagram and press, not a website.

From Daily Hive and write-ups

Organised by how tired you are.

The in-house menu is grouped in playful categories rather than a standard coffee-and-tea list. Drink names below come from Daily Hive’s visit (18 September 2026) and may change or sell out — the Sweet Corn Matcha was already gone by 5 PM that day.

Matcha

Sweet corn & black sesame

The viral Sweet Corn Matcha, plus a black sesame matcha latte with cold foam. Sweetness can be adjusted; staff suggested 40% in the review visit.

Espresso

When you’re super tired

Espresso drinks live under that heading. Write-ups also mention an Iced Banana Bread Latte (espresso or matcha) and a Calamansi Espresso Spritz.

Bakes

Pastries on the side

Sourdough croissants from Freya’s in Campbell River, and other bakes from To Live For Bakery, have been served at the counter.
