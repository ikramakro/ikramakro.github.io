Newington

A lunch board that deserves a home page.

Fettle is at 27–29 Marshall Street, Edinburgh EH8 9BJ, close to the university. Edinburgh Live covered the opening as a wood-fired sourdough focaccia and salad spot. Sluurpy lists a phone and address but no official website; OpenStreetMap had Instagram and Facebook only. A directory subdomain (fettle.uk-restaurants.com) is not an owned site.

From press and public menus

Focaccia, salads, and coffee.

Press and public menus describe wood-fired sourdough focaccia, salads, sandwiches, coffee, and desserts. Fillings mentioned on a 2026 directory menu include goat’s cheese, hummus and falafel, pastrami and emmental, chicken pesto, chicken bacon and avocado, honey ham and emmental, grilled halloumi, vegan “tuna,” and home-cured beetroot salmon. Treat that as a snapshot — the daily board can change, and prices are not listed here.
