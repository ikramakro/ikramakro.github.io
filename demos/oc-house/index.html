Family-run, late night

A long seafood menu that shouldn’t live only on Facebook.

Ốc House serves Southern Vietnamese street-style seafood in a casual room on SE 82nd Avenue. Reviewers mention generous grilled squid, hotpots, and a welcoming family-run feel. The official presence is Facebook, plus delivery apps — this demo gathers hours, the address, and a sample of the public menu in one place.
