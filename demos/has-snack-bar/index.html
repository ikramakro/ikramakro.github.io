Shoebox-sized, much loved

Built for people who are not already in the know.

Ha’s Snack Bar is run by chefs Anthony Ha and Sadie Mae Burns, who cooked as the Ha’s Đặc Biệt pop-up before opening this room in January 2025. The menu is a chalkboard that changes daily. Press often mentions snails in garlic-tamarind butter, oysters with green chili, chicken liver pâté, a sweetbread vol-au-vent, curried lobster, and onion soup — but the dish you read about may not be on that night. There is no phone number and no standalone website. News is shared on the owners’ Instagram, @has_dac_biet.

Getting in

Resy first, walk-ins if you can.

Details below come from the restaurant’s Resy page, the Instagram bio, and recent press (including SeatSwiper, October 2026). They may change — check Resy on the day.

Book

Reservations on Resy only

There is no second booking form. Tables are limited (press has cited a room of about 24), and reservations often go quickly. Recent sources say the book opens around three weeks ahead at noon — confirm on the live Resy calendar.

Walk-ins

Walk-ins welcomed

The Resy page says walk-ins are welcomed. Parties of 2–3 have 1.5 hours to dine; parties of 4 or more have 2 hours. The Infatuation has suggested arriving early for the first seating.

Menu

A chalkboard that changes

Popular dishes in the press are only a snapshot. Everything on the Instagram bio is noted as containing fish sauce — important if fish is an allergy.
