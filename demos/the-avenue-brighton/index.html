Neighbourhood cafe

Not flashy — just a well-loved morning table on St James’s Street.

The Avenue is a Kemptown cafe known for breakfast, brunch, and all-day plates, with plants, natural light, and outdoor seating. Reviewers mention good coffee, generous food, and a friendly room, though it can be hard to find a seat at busy times. Reservations and catering have been mentioned on public listings.
